# Lead follow-up

Prepare one external action at a time. The Hub is the record of context and
history; Chrome is only the signed-in surface where the owner acts.

## Workflow

1. Open the selected row at `/studio/leads` and read its objective, note,
   language, next action, and source.
2. Open the saved destination in the approved Chrome tab.
3. Let the owner read the current rules and decide whether the action is
   allowed and useful.
4. Prepare at most one contextual follow, membership request, reply, or
   message. Stop before the final click and ask the owner to confirm.
5. After the owner acts, record the exact result in the Hub with one event and
   update the relation/contact status.

## Evidence

Return the destination, action proposed, human decision, result, timestamp, and
the next action. If the page is unavailable, private, or its rules prohibit the
action, record that fact and leave the external surface unchanged.

Do not automate clicks, use bulk actions, send repeated messages, disguise
automation with timing, bypass platform limits, or call a social API.
