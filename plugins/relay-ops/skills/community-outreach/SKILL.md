# Community outreach

Adapt one message to one community or profile. Start from the Hub voice
examples and the destination’s context; do not cross-post the same copy.

## Workflow

1. Read the piste’s objective, platform, language, rules note, and next action.
2. Load the approved editorial voice and the relevant product facts.
3. Draft one short message with one clear request and no unsupported claim.
4. Identify the rule or fact that still needs checking.
5. Save the draft in the Hub or copy it into the prepared browser surface only
   after the owner approves the destination and wording.
6. Stop before send. After a human sends it, log `message_sent` and update the
   contact status; otherwise keep `draft_ready`.

## Output

```text
Destination:
Language:
Context:
Draft:
Rule or fact to confirm:
Approval: needs human review
```

Do not mass-message, repeat a campaign across groups, invent permission or
partnership, contact private members, or publish on behalf of the owner.
